About Zenith

Local, multi-brand, and hands-on since 1971

Zenith has operated under the Zenith name in Navsari since 1971, across businesses involving appliances, electronics and kitchenware. Today, Zenith Navsari brings together two sides of that same trade: repair and servicing of home appliances, and sales of electronics, appliances and kitchenware for your home.

Our own technicians carry out repairs — at your home or at our store/workshop — across multiple brands of washing machines, refrigerators, air conditioners, TVs, ovens, microwaves, geysers, gas stoves, chimneys and more. We're a multi-brand repair business, not an authorized service centre for any one brand.

  • Own technicians for home visits and workshop repairs
  • Multi-brand — washing machines and refrigerators are our strongest repair categories
  • Also a retail store for electronics, home appliances and kitchenware
  • Serving Navsari city and nearby villages
