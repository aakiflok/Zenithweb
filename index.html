About Zenith

Local, multi-brand, and hands-on since 1971

Zenith has operated under the Zenith name in Navsari since 1971, across businesses involving appliances, electronics and kitchenware. Today, Zenith Navsari brings together two sides of that same trade: repair and servicing of home appliances, and sales of electronics, appliances and kitchenware for your home.

Our own technicians carry out repairs — at your home or at our store/workshop — across multiple brands of washing machines, refrigerators, air conditioners, TVs, ovens, microwaves, geysers, gas stoves, chimneys and more. We're a multi-brand repair business, not an authorized service centre for any one brand.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which appliances do you repair?

We repair washing machines, refrigerators, air conditioners, TVs, ovens, microwaves, geysers and water heaters, gas stoves, chimneys, and other home appliances and electronics, subject to confirmation. Washing machines and refrigerators are our strongest repair categories.

Do you provide home visits?

Yes. Our technicians carry out home visits, and repairs can also be done at our store/workshop depending on the appliance and issue.

Is there a visiting or inspection charge?

Visiting/inspection charges apply. Contact us for details.

How soon can service be arranged?

We aim to arrange service within approximately 1–2 days, depending on technician availability, your location and the problem. Earlier service may sometimes be possible, but we don't guarantee same-day service.

Are you an authorized brand service centre?

We are a multi-brand repair business and are not an authorized service centre for any particular brand.

Can I enquire about buying a new appliance?

Yes. Contact our store for available brands, models, current prices and availability of refrigerators, air conditioners, TVs, washing machines, ovens, gas stoves, chimneys, geysers, kitchenware and more.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Navsari city and nearby villages.

What are your opening hours?

Monday – Saturday, 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM; Sunday, 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM (Asia/Kolkata).
